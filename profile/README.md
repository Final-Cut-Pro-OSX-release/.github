## Why It Happens

Nearly every entry above traces back to one design decision. The Magnetic Timeline uses advanced
metadata rather than fixed tracks, so clips relate to each other instead of occupying reserved lanes.
Editors who learned on track-based systems expect material to stay where it was dropped; here it moves
to stay in sync. Once Clip Connections and Compound Clips become reflex, the same behaviour that felt
unpredictable turns into the reason final cut pro for mac edits faster than the alternatives.

The performance complaints have a similar root. Content Auto-Analysis captures camera metadata and
analyses shots in the background, which is invaluable later but active while you work. Combined with
uncompressed high-resolution source files, that can look like a slow application when it is really a
storage and bandwidth question that proxies solve outright.
